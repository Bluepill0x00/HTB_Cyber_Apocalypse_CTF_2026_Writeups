## Storyline & Theme

> This is no longer a war of blades — it is a war of **infrastructure, logic, and counterfeit governance**.
> 
> Ambitious lords weaponize forged paperwork and buy off city checkpoints, while a far more terrifying threat marches from the fog: the Quiet Marches. Led by the enigmatic Alyss, this mindless "Hollow Host" uses a highly synchronized, unbreathing cadence to process entire villages into silent compliance.
> 
> The endgame is not a throne of absolute power, but the creation of **The Salt Crown** — a fault-tolerant constraint system designed to put a permanent leash on authority. The system will only empower a leader who accepts the leash. Secure the chain, or inherit the ruins.

## AI Rules of Engagement

This event allowed AI as a **supporting tool**:

- **AI tools allowed as aids:** Use AI assistants to support your thinking. Solving a challenge with a single prompt isn't in the spirit of the event.
- **Be ready to explain your approach:** Event hosts may ask how you solved a challenge; be prepared to give a brief walkthrough of your reasoning when requested.
- **Misuse may cost your solves:** If hosts find AI was used as the primary solver, your solves may be revoked and your score adjusted.
